Remarks

  • Data Period: Jun 2025 – May 2026 (1-26) (12 months). Data starts Jun 2025 (no May'25 baseline).
  • Excluded Orders: Cancelled and Returned orders are excluded from all calculations. Only successfully completed/shipped orders are counted.
  • Revenue: Represents the total order value (including taxes, shipping) as recorded by the seller platform. This is gross revenue, not net of returns.
  • AOV (Avg Order Value): Calculated as Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders. This is a weighted average across the period — not an average of monthly AOVs.
  • MoM %: Month-over-Month percentage change in revenue compared to the previous month. First month shows "—" as no prior month is available.
  • Geography Data: Top 15 states and Top 20 cities are shown. Minor cities/states are excluded from tables but included in totals.
  • Discount: Total discount given off the MRP across all orders. Higher discounts may correlate with promotional campaigns or B2B pricing.
  • Coffee Machine: This product has a significantly higher AOV (~₹8,730) compared to capsule products (~₹550–₹2,300) due to its hardware nature.
  • New Products: Some products (Boost & Buzz, Hazelnut Cream, Bold & Balanced, Caramel Swirl) launched mid-period and have partial-period data.
  • Currency: All monetary values are in Indian Rupees (₹) with Indian numeral formatting (K = Thousands, L = Lakhs, Cr = Crores).

Glossary

TermDefinition
RevenueTotal sales value from completed orders in Indian Rupees (₹).
OrdersCount of individual order transactions placed by customers.
Qty / UnitsTotal quantity of individual product units sold. One order can contain multiple units.
AOVAverage Order Value = Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders. Indicates spending per transaction.
MoM %Month-over-Month growth rate. Green (▲) = increase, Red (▼) = decrease vs. previous month.
% RevPercentage share of total revenue. Indicates how much each item contributes to overall sales.
DiscountTotal monetary discount applied across all orders, calculated as difference from MRP.
ZoneGeographic region of India — North, South, East, West — based on delivery address.
TierCity classification — Tier 1 (metros), Tier 2 (large cities), Tier 3 (smaller towns).
Capsule TypeCategory grouping of coffee products — Speciality, Variety Pack, Explorer's, Single Origin, Flavoured, or Machine.
FBAFulfilled by Amazon — orders shipped from Amazon warehouses for faster delivery.
FBMFulfilled by Merchant — orders shipped directly by the seller (Elevens Coffee).
SKUStock Keeping Unit — a unique product identifier. In this dashboard, each product name acts as an SKU.
CSV ExportClick the "CSV" button on any table to download its data as a spreadsheet-compatible file.
