Remarks

  1. Data Source: Amazon Ads console export — Sponsored Products, Sponsored Display, and Sponsored Brands targeting reports.
  2. Data Period: Jun 17, 2025 – Mar 31, 2026 (~10 months). SP and SD data starts from Jun 2025; SB starts from Sep 2025.
  3. Attribution Window: All sales and orders use Amazon's 14-day attribution model ("14 Day Total Sales/Orders").
  4. Period Labels: Original data used custom period labels ("Learning period", "1 month", etc.) which have been mapped to calendar months for consistency.
  5. ROAS Calculation: ROAS = Total Sales / Total Spend (weighted, not averaged across rows). ACOS = Total Spend / Total Sales × 100.
  6. Product Types: "Pods" (coffee capsules), "Machines" (brewing machines), "Pods+Machine" (SB campaigns covering both).
  7. New-to-Brand (NTB): Available for SD and SB only. Represents customers buying the brand for the first time via ads.
  8. SP Targeting: Includes Auto (Close/Loose/Complements/Substitutes) and Manual (Brand/Category/Competitor keywords + product ASINs).
  9. SB Started Later: Sponsored Brands campaigns launched in Sep 2025 (3 months after SP/SD), so SB has fewer months of data.
  10. Currency: All monetary values are in Indian Rupees (₹). Formatted as K (thousands), L (lakhs), Cr (crores).

Glossary

Spend — Total advertising cost paid to Amazon
Sales (14d) — Revenue attributed to ads within 14-day window
ROAS — Return on Ad Spend = Sales / Spend
ACOS — Advertising Cost of Sale = Spend / Sales × 100%
Impressions — Number of times ads were shown
Clicks — Number of times ads were clicked
CTR — Click-Through Rate = Clicks / Impressions × 100%
CPC — Cost Per Click = Spend / Clicks
CPM — Cost Per Mille = Spend / Impressions × 1000
Orders — Purchases attributed to ads (14-day window)
NTB — New-to-Brand: first-time brand purchasers via ads
SP — Sponsored Products (keyword/ASIN targeted product ads)
SD — Sponsored Display (audience/product retargeting ads)
SB — Sponsored Brands (banner/video brand awareness ads)
DPV — Detail Page Views (product page visits from ads)
