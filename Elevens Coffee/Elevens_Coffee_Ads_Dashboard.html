Where did the change come from?

How the numbers are worked out: ROAS, ACOS, CTR, CPC, CPM and conversion rate are recalculated from the period totals (for example ROAS = total ad sales ÷ total ad spend), never averaged day by day. Per day = period total ÷ calendar days in the period, so periods of different length can be compared fairly; ratios are the same in both views. Green = better, orange = worse; spend has no colour because more or less spend is a choice, not a result.

Remarks

  1. Data Source: Amazon Ads console export — Sponsored Products, Sponsored Display, and Sponsored Brands targeting reports.
  2. Data Period: Jun 17, 2025 – Nov 10, 2026 (~17 months). SP and SD data starts from Jun 2025; SB starts from Sep 2025. Nov 2026 = 1–10; ad sales for recent days keep filling under the 14-day attribution window. Spend includes all Amazon ad accounts.
  3. Attribution Window: All ad sales and orders use Amazon's 14-day attribution model. From Oct 2026 the reports come from Amazon's new console export, which does not label the window; the main ad account matches the 14-day figures and the smaller account is used as reported. "Ad Sales (14d)" = revenue attributed to ads within 14 days of a customer interacting with the ad.
  4. Period Labels: Original data used custom period labels ("Learning period", "1 month", etc.) which have been mapped to calendar months for consistency.
  5. Ad ROAS Calculation: Ad ROAS = Ad Sales (14d) / Ad Spend (weighted, not averaged across rows). ACOS = Ad Spend / Ad Sales (14d) x 100.
  6. CPC vs CPM: SP and SB ads use Cost-Per-Click (CPC) pricing. SD ads use viewable CPM (vCPM) pricing — both CPC and CPM are shown for SD.
  7. Product Types: "Pods" (coffee capsules), "Machines" (brewing machines), "Pods+Machine" (SB campaigns covering both).
  8. New-to-Brand (NTB): Available for SD and SB only. Represents customers buying the brand for the first time via ads.
  9. SP Targeting: Includes Auto (Close/Loose/Complements/Substitutes) and Manual (Brand/Category/Competitor keywords + product ASINs).
  10. SB Started Later: Sponsored Brands campaigns launched in Sep 2025 (3 months after SP/SD), so SB has fewer months of data.
  11. Currency: All monetary values are in Indian Rupees (₹). Formatted as K (thousands), L (lakhs), Cr (crores).

Glossary

Ad Spend — Total advertising cost paid to Amazon
Ad Sales (14d) — Revenue attributed to ads within 14-day attribution window after customer interacts with the ad
Ad ROAS — Return on Ad Spend = Ad Sales (14d) / Ad Spend
bROAS — Blended ROAS = Total Retail Sales / Ad Spend (not shown in this dashboard)
ACOS — Advertising Cost of Sale = Ad Spend / Ad Sales (14d) x 100%
Impressions — Number of times ads were shown
Clicks — Number of times ads were clicked
CTR — Click-Through Rate = Clicks / Impressions x 100%
CPC — Cost Per Click = Ad Spend / Clicks (primary metric for SP & SB)
CPM — Cost Per 1,000 Impressions = Ad Spend / Impressions x 1000 (relevant for SD)
Orders — Purchases attributed to ads (14-day window)
NTB — New-to-Brand: first-time brand purchasers via ads
SP — Sponsored Products (keyword/ASIN targeted product ads, CPC pricing)
SD — Sponsored Display (audience/product retargeting ads, vCPM pricing)
SB — Sponsored Brands (banner/video brand awareness ads, CPC pricing)
DPV — Detail Page Views (product page visits from ads)
Disclaimer: Reported metrics reflect gross platform activity based on a 14-day attribution window and provide a real-time performance overview. These figures may differ from final invoiced totals as they are subject to Amazon's automated traffic validation process. Invalid traffic (IVT) and non-billable events are identified and removed by Amazon for accurate financial reporting.
