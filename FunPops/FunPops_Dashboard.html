Glossary & Key Definitions

GMVGross Merchandise Value — total selling price of items sold (used by Blinkit & Zepto)
Net SalesRevenue after returns, discounts, and fees (used by Amazon — Invoice Amount)
Ad-SpendTotal money invested in platform advertising during the period
Ad-RevenueSales attributed to ads by the platform (attribution window varies: Amazon 14-day, others real-time)
ROASReturn on Ad Spend = Ad-Revenue ÷ Ad-Spend. ≥3x Strong | 1–3x Moderate | <1x Below breakeven
bROASBlended ROAS = Ad-Revenue ÷ Total Platform Sales. Measures how much of your total sales ads are driving
CPCCost Per Click = Ad-Spend ÷ Total Clicks. Used by Amazon, Zepto, BigBasket (click-based pricing)
CPMCost Per Mille = Ad-Spend per 1,000 impressions. Used by Blinkit (impression-based pricing)
CTRClick-Through Rate = (Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100. Measures ad engagement
ATCAdd to Cart — number of times users added the product to cart after viewing an ad
% of ActualPercentage of total actual ad-spend from billing. Detail-level data may not sum to 100% due to attribution gaps
SP / SB / SDSponsored Products / Sponsored Brands / Sponsored Display — ad types on Amazon & Zepto
Platform-specific notes: Amazon sales = Net Invoice Amount (Shipments only, excl. refunds/replacements) | Blinkit GMV = Qty × MRP (₹240/box) | Zepto sales = GMV from partner dashboard | BigBasket sales = Sales Report (New) total_sales | Keyword-level spend may differ from billing actuals by 3–6% due to attribution windows and non-search placements.
