Why this process exists, and who runs it

Clients pay NorthSide to know their numbers and grow them. That only works if every brand's data moves the same way, every week, no matter who is handling it. One broken link in the chain = a client seeing wrong or late numbers.

TRACK A — WEEKLY / BIWEEKLYShikha — Data & DSR ownerLogs into every platform panel, downloads daily sales / stock / spend, updates the live DSR, shares it with the client, and files everything on the brand's Google Drive.
THE RECORD STOREGoogle Drive — brand-level linksThe single shared home for every raw file and working sheet. Upload after every download; download from here for any analysis. If it's not on Drive, it didn't happen.
TRACK B — MONTHLY + MID-MONTHKAM — Analysis & AI ownerAkash Thakur / Akash Gandhi / Shikha (per brand). Downloads granular ads reports, keeps the brand's Claude folder current, builds the HTML dashboards and the clean Excel data file.

The clean Excel — 6 standard segmentations

One segmented, analysis-ready Excel per brand, maintained by Claude, updated on every data drop. Same 6 columns for every KAM, every brand — so any analysis is comparable across the agency.

1 · City Tier (Sales)Every city mapped to Tier 1 / 2 / 3 from the master City_Tier_Mapping file in Templates/. One mapping for the whole agency — never improvised per brand.
2 · Flavor / Pack Size (Sales)Product attributes per the brand's own catalog (flavor, pack size, format — whatever that brand sells by).
3 · Campaign Type (Ads)Every campaign bucketed into broad standardized types — Banner/Display · Keyword/Search-term · Remarketing · Category targeting · Product targeting/Contextual — the same buckets on every platform, whatever the platform calls them (matrix below).
4 · BCG keyword type (Ads)Every keyword tagged Brand / Competitor / Generic. This is the backbone of ads storytelling: brand terms defend, competitor terms attack, generic terms grow.
5 · Negative-BCG flag (Ads, separate column)Terms not relevant to the product directly or indirectly.KAM-ONLY — for optimisation reference · never shown on dashboards
6 · Keyword framework ring (Ads, separate column from BCG)Which ring of the "search words to invest behind" framework the keyword sits in — see below. Investment priority flows from the core outward.

The keyword framework — developing search words to invest behind

  • Core Search Words — what a shopper types when they want exactly this product. Fund these first, always.
  • Direct Competitors · Close Substitutes · Alternates — shoppers about to buy something like ours. Second priority.
  • Complements — products bought alongside ours; catches shoppers in the same basket mission.
  • Specialists — different products that can meet the same need; the widest, most experimental ring.

Framework ring and BCG are two separate columns on every keyword — BCG says whose term it is; the ring says how close it sits to our product.

Quality checks — before anything reaches the client

1 · Totals matchDashboard and clean-file totals reconcile with the raw files and the DSR. Every difference is explained (a known basis difference) or escalated — never ignored.
2 · Spot re-checkRe-calculate a few headline numbers straight from the raw files and compare. If a total was computed once and reused everywhere, it's untested until you do this.
3 · Looks right everywhereClick every tab and filter. No blanks, no "NaN", no placeholder text, no chart that quietly failed to draw.
4 · Locked & liveClient dashboard password works and wrong passwords are rejected. If hosted online, open the LIVE page and check it — an upload that "succeeded" is not proof the page updated.

Glossary — for anyone new

DSR — Daily Sales Report; our live topline sheet for the client.
GMV — Gross Merchandise Value; sales value at MRP.
Ad-Spend / Ad-Revenue — money spent on ads / sales directly from ads.
Ad-ROAS — Ad-Revenue ÷ Ad-Spend. How many ₹ of ad sales per ₹1 spent.
bROAS — Blended ROAS: TOTAL sales ÷ Ad-Spend (organic included).
Keyword — the words a shopper types in search; we bid on these.
Campaign — a group of ads with one budget and goal.
SP / SB / SD — Sponsored Products / Brands / Display: the main ad formats.
Remarketing — ads shown again to people who saw or bought before.
BCG — Brand / Competitor / Generic keyword tagging.
Negative keyword — a term we don't want our ads to show for.
Dark store — a q-commerce mini-warehouse that serves one area.
City Tier — Tier 1 metros, Tier 2 large cities, Tier 3 the rest — from the master mapping.
Panel — a platform's seller/advertiser login (Seller Central, Blinkit Ads, etc.).
KAM — Key Account Manager; owns a brand end-to-end.
