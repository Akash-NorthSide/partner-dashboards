NorthSide Strategic Recommendations

Data-driven strategies to accelerate sales growth and expand market presence across platforms.

Quick Win

Activate Tier-2 City Push

Cities like Jaipur, Lucknow, and Chandigarh show 3x higher growth rates than metros. Launch targeted promotions with platform-specific coupons to capture early market share.

Expected Impact: +₹15-20L monthly revenue within 45 days
High Impact

Bundle Strategy for AOV Growth

Customers buying 2+ SKUs have 45% higher lifetime value. Create curated bundles with 10-15% discount to drive cross-category purchases on Blinkit and Zepto.

Expected Impact: +22% AOV increase, +₹180 per order
Strategic

Zepto-First New Launch Strategy

Zepto's rapid growth (45% MoM) and younger demographics make it ideal for new product launches. Exclusive 7-day launch windows with in-app visibility can drive 3x trial rates.

Expected Impact: 40% faster product adoption vs multi-platform launch
Quick Win

Optimize Instamart Visibility

Category ranking on Instamart correlates with 2.8x more organic orders. Improve listing quality — better images, enhanced descriptions, and keyword optimization.

Expected Impact: 25-30% organic order growth in 3 weeks
High Impact

Weekend Flash Sale Calendar

Weekend orders are 35% higher than weekdays. Implement a rotating weekend flash sale program across platforms with coordinated inventory and promotional pricing.

Expected Impact: +18% weekend revenue, improved inventory turns
Strategic

Subscription & Repeat Purchase Program

Only 12% of customers reorder within 30 days. Launch auto-replenishment options on Amazon and subscribe-and-save bundles to build predictable recurring revenue.

Expected Impact: 35-50% increase in repeat purchase rate over 90 days
