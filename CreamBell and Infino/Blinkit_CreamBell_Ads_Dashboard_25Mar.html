Key Learnings — CreamBell Blinkit Ads (Mar 1–24)

  • Category Targeting delivers best ROAS (3.97x): ₹4.11L spend generating ₹16.32L GMV — category-level placements in Sticks, Cups, Cones drive strong returns.
  • Brand keywords outperform massively (7.88x ROAS): "cream bell" (6.93x) and "creambell" (24.48x) show strong brand search intent on Blinkit.
  • Retargeting Recommendations deliver 9.51x ROAS: Repeat order suggestions to existing users convert extremely well. Scale this channel.
  • Keyword Targeting is largest spend but lowest ROAS (2.07x): "ice cream" alone consumes ₹4.59L at 0.96x ROAS — major optimization needed.
  • Indirect sales dominate (₹27.92L vs ₹14.37L direct): Blinkit ads generate significant halo effect beyond direct attribution.
  • 23,607 new users acquired: CreamBell ads driving meaningful customer acquisition across all targeting types.

Blinkit Campaign Learnings

  • Retargeting Reco is the star campaign (9.53x ROAS): ₹53.4K spend generating ₹5.09L GMV — repeat order suggestions deliver exceptional returns.
  • Category Targeting is the efficiency leader (3.97x ROAS): ₹4.11L spend across 4 sub-categories captures high-intent ice cream shoppers.
  • Generic Keywords need restructuring: ₹5.55L at only 1.22x ROAS — broad "ice cream" keyword is the biggest drag.
  • Brand Keywords highly profitable (7.73x ROAS): Only ₹83.2K spend — significantly under-invested. Scale brand keyword budgets.
  • Audience Reco (1.73x) vs Retargeting (9.53x): Retargeting existing users far more efficient than new user targeting.

Targeting Deep Dive Learnings

  • Sticks is the top category (4.54x ROAS): ₹1.65L spend generating ₹7.49L GMV — impulse stick products convert strongly.
  • Single Serve Cups closely follow (3.98x): Lower ASP, high repeat purchase frequency converts efficiently.
  • Retargeting is #1 recommendation asset (9.51x ROAS): ₹53.6K for ₹5.09L GMV — most efficient channel overall.
  • Cart Recommendations weakest at 1.10x ROAS: ₹2.22L spend with low return — needs bid optimization.
  • Indirect Sales are 2x Direct across all types: Strong halo effect from Blinkit ads.

Keyword Strategy Learnings

  • "ice cream" dominates generic spend: ₹4.59L at 0.96x ROAS — 72% of keyword budget doesn’t break even. Restructure with specific terms.
  • "cream bell" brand keyword delivers 6.93x ROAS: ₹57.4K generating ₹3.98L GMV — highly profitable brand search.
  • "creambell" at 24.48x ROAS: Only ₹3.3K spend but extraordinary efficiency — exact brand match captures high-intent buyers.
  • Brand (7.88x) vs Generic (1.26x): 6x efficiency gap — shift 20-30% of generic budget to brand keywords.
  • Long-tail generics mixed: "kulfi" (2.67x), "ice cream cake" (3.68x) perform well; "ice" (1.49x) underperforms.
