Key Learnings — CreamBell Instamart Ads (Mar 2–24)

  • Auto Suggest dominates spend (53%): ₹3.51L spent with 1.71x ROAS — highest absolute GMV (₹6.00L) but efficiency is moderate. Consider optimizing bids to improve returns.
  • Guaranteed Top Slot is the best ROAS performer: 5.30x ROAS on just ₹5.8K spend — extremely efficient. Consider scaling budget on this ad type for high-intent visibility.
  • Browse Boost delivers solid returns: 1.89x ROAS with highest A2C count (13,660) — strong product discovery but conversion rate needs improvement.
  • Keyword Based Ads underperform: 0.49x ROAS on ₹1.16L spend — CPC keyword campaigns (ItemAds) show particularly poor returns (0.16x). Smart Bids keywords (1.63x) are significantly better.
  • YGTI and Last minute add-ons show low efficiency: Both below 0.70x ROAS — review targeting strategy for these placements.
  • Tubs lead product revenue: Caramel Butterscotch Tub is the top performer (₹74.8K GMV, 1.79x ROAS) followed by French Vanilla and Choco Brownie tubs.

Instamart Advertising Learnings

  • Auto Suggest is the highest-spend placement: ₹3.51L spend capturing 55% of total budget — banner-style auto-suggest drives volume but at moderate 1.71x ROAS.
  • Guaranteed Top Slot punches above its weight: Only ₹5.8K spend but 5.30x ROAS — top slot keywords convert extremely well. Increase budget allocation here.
  • Smart Bids campaign drives multiple ad types: Browse Boost (1.89x), YGTI (0.66x), Keywords (1.63x), Last minute add-ons (0.49x) — consider splitting into separate campaigns for better control.
  • ItemAds Keywords CPC is inefficient: 0.16x ROAS on ₹90K spend — this is the biggest drag on overall performance. Pause or significantly restructure.
  • Tubs dominate product GMV: Top 3 products are all tubs (Caramel Butterscotch, French Vanilla, Choco Brownie) — consumers prefer large-format products on Instamart.
  • Cakes show good ROAS: Black Forest Cake (1.37x) and Double Chocolate Cake (1.11x) — premium celebration products convert well.

City-Level Learnings

  • Mumbai leads GMV (₹1.11L) with 1.64x ROAS: Best balance of volume and efficiency among top metros. Strong organic demand for CreamBell.
  • Delhi delivers highest efficiency among top cities: 1.85x ROAS on ₹58K spend — metro consumers respond well to ads.
  • Hyderabad is highest spend but moderate ROAS (1.19x): ₹69.8K spend — investigate if competitive pressure is driving up costs.
  • Bangalore underperforms at 0.75x ROAS: ₹68K spend yielding only ₹51K GMV — significant budget optimization needed.
  • Gurgaon and Noida show strong efficiency: Gurgaon (2.00x) and Noida 1 (2.04x) — satellite cities outperform parent metros.
  • 122 cities active: Consider consolidating budget to top 30–40 cities for better ROI, as long-tail cities have fragmented spend.

Keyword Strategy Learnings

  • "ice creams" is the dominant keyword: ₹4.37L spend (92% of keyword budget) at 1.38x ROAS — broad category keyword captures high volume but moderate efficiency.
  • Brand keywords are highly efficient: 5.09x ROAS across 52 brand keywords — but only ₹6K spend. Consider increasing brand keyword budgets significantly.
  • "creambell" brand keyword delivers 5.66x ROAS: Strongest individual keyword — brand searches convert extremely well on Instamart.
  • Competitor keywords show moderate returns: "amul rajbhog" (2.39x) and "havmor" (6.15x) perform well — selective competitor conquesting works.
  • "hocco" and "aamchi mango ice cream" show 0x ROAS: Zero GMV on these competitor/generic keywords — consider pausing.
  • Flavour-specific generics perform well: "vanilla ice cream" (1.90x), "ice creams vanilla" (9.45x) — specific flavour keywords show strong purchase intent.
