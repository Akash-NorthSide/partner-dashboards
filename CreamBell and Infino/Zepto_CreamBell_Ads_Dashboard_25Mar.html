Key Learnings — CreamBell Zepto Ads (Mar 1–24)

  • Sponsored Display outperforms SP: SD delivers 4.56x ROAS vs 3.99x for SP, indicating strong returns from cross-category and brand display placements.
  • Cakes dominate revenue: Cakes, Sandwiches & More sub-category accounts for the largest share of ad-revenue, led by Double Chocolate Ice Cream Cake (500ml) at 4.83x ROAS.
  • Generic keywords deliver best efficiency: Generic keywords show 4.20x ROAS vs 3.97x for Brand and 2.63x for Competitor — indicating category-level discovery drives stronger returns.
  • Competitor keywords show low efficiency: 289 competitor keywords yielding only 2.63x ROAS — consider reducing competitor keyword spend or optimizing bids.
  • "ice cream cake" is a hero keyword: 7.85x ROAS at high volume — consider increasing bids on cake-related generics.

Zepto Advertising Learnings

  • SD Cross-Category is the strongest placement: Highest revenue (₹5.21L) and best ROAS (4.71x) — cross-category targeting exposes CreamBell products to impulse ice cream buyers browsing other categories.
  • Automatic targeting is cost-efficient: SP Automatic drives highest SP impressions (5.75L) at lowest CPM (₹171) — let Zepto's algorithm discover high-intent audiences.
  • Cakes are the hero SKU group: Double Chocolate Cake alone generates ₹3.01L revenue at 4.83x ROAS — premium cakes have strong ad-driven purchase intent.
  • Tubs show highest ROAS (4.02x): Higher ASP products convert well — consider increasing Tub ad budgets, especially Belgian Chocolate and Mango which show 10x+ ROAS.
  • Only 1 SD campaign still ACTIVE (Product Display :: Brand): 4 SP + 1 SD campaigns are PAUSED — ensure paused campaigns are intentional and not impacting reach.
  • Competitor keyword spend is minimal (₹8.4K): Low ROAS (2.63x) suggests competitor conquesting is not efficient. Brand + Generic keywords should be prioritized.

City-Level Learnings

  • Mumbai leads in both revenue and ROAS: ₹4.22L revenue at 5.01x ROAS — strong organic demand combined with efficient ad spend in the metro.
  • Tier-1 metros dominate: Mumbai + Delhi account for ~35% of total ad-revenue. Focus budgets on proven high-revenue cities.
  • Bengaluru underperforms on ROAS (2.75x): Despite decent revenue (₹1.11L), efficiency is below average — investigate competitive pressure or audience-product mismatch.
  • Emerging cities show strong efficiency: Gurugram (5.15x), Sas Nagar (5.18x), Nashik (5.24x), and Ghaziabad (5.04x) — consider scaling budgets in these high-ROAS cities.
  • Long-tail cities have low spend but variable ROAS: 63 cities active — consider consolidating budget to top 20-25 cities for better ROI.

Keyword Strategy Learnings

  • "creambell" brand keyword: Largest single keyword spend (₹61.4K) at 3.88x ROAS — brand searches are strong but ensure brand bidding is not cannibalizing organic traffic.
  • "ice cream cake" is the best-performing generic: 7.85x ROAS at significant volume — this is a high-intent category keyword driving CreamBell cake purchases.
  • Category generics perform well: "butterscotch ice cream" (4.80x), "chocolate ice cream" (4.22x), "caramel" (5.33x) — flavour-specific keywords show strong purchase intent.
  • Broad generics have lower ROAS: "ice" (3.79x), "ice cream" (3.50x) — high volume but less efficient. Consider shifting budget to more specific generics.
  • Competitor "cornetto" keyword: 2.56x ROAS on ₹3.3K spend — not efficient. Competitor conquesting should be minimal unless strategic.
