What each lever is worth

One input changed at a time. ★ marks a change that moves the pack into a different size tier.
Grocery & Gourmet Food charges 8% referral at or below $15.00 and 15% above it — applied to the whole price, not just the part above $15. One cent over the line costs about $1.05, which creates a band of prices just above $15 that earn less than $15.00 does.
Grocery is one of the few categories that works this way. Most use marginal rates, where each rate applies only to the slice of price inside its band — those have no cliff.

Size tiers

A pack lands in the first tier it satisfies on every axis. Breaching any single limit pushes it up a tier. Amazon bills on shipping weight — the greater of actual weight and length × width × height ÷ 139.

Fulfilment rates — non-peak, before the 3.5% surcharge

Large Standard is the tier 500 of the 586 scanned listings fall into. Small Bulky covers the other common shape — tall multipack cartons.

Method & limits

Four things are specific to a seller account and appear as settings rather than being detected: Low-Price FBA enrolment, SIPP certification, which inbound placement option is chosen, and any New Selection discounts. Category assignment is also Amazon's call — a listing can sit in a different referral category than expected, so confirm before building a launch plan on a specific percentage.

Inbound placement is a manual input rather than a calculated figure: published rates for it diverge too far to model honestly. Guidance for standard-size packs is $0.14–$0.70 per unit, and Amazon-Optimized Splits are $0.

Amazon revises fulfilment fees each January and switches to peak rates on 15 October. The rate card version is shown at the top right. When those dates pass the tables need refreshing; the arithmetic does not.
